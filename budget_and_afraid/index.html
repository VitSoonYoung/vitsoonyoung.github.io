Privacy Policy

Data Collection and Use

Budget and Afraid does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data entered within the app remains stored locally on your device and is not shared with any third parties.

Data Storage

All budget information and preferences are stored locally on your device. This data is never uploaded to any server or shared with external services.

Third-Party Services

Budget and Afraid does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data from your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Budget and Afraid is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
